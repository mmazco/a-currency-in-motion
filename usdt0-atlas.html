USDT0 on Stellar Network

A currency in motion shouldn’t be limited by its network

By now you or your users probably hold some amount of USDT on one or multiple networks. USDT0 connects Stellar into a unified USDT supply, so balances move across networks without fragmentation. Here it arrives as a native Stellar asset compatible with your existing Stellar stack.

What that makes possible here, and not elsewhere

Unified liquidity

One USDT0 supply, shared across the 28 networks it connects. Liquidity can arrive from where it already sits, rather than having to be built up on Stellar first.

Movement without fragmentation

Balances move into and out of Stellar without a custodial bridge or a wrapped representation. Fees are LayerZero messaging costs, quoted per route before you send.

Simpler integrations

Payment flows, DeFi applications and treasury systems build against one consistent USDT asset. The SAC is already deployed, so contracts reach USDT0 through the normal token interface.

Deploy — adding USDT0 to your environment Live on Stellar Mainnet

USDT0 is live on Stellar Mainnet. Add it to your wallet or environment. Test your integration by moving a small amount end to end before rolling out to your users.
