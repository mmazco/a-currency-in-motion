Where the dollars come from

Every USDT0 is backed by USDT locked on Ethereum. From there it moves chain to chain by burning at one end and minting at the other, and Stellar is one of those ends. This follows the numbered flow in the USDT0 docs, with Stellar as the destination.

Proposed — B
What changed from A. The two-path version put Ethereum and another chain side by side as alternatives, which lost where the supply comes from. This shows the chain: USDT locked on Ethereum, minted onto a USDT0 chain, moved to Stellar, and unlocked again if it goes back. Same three interactions the docs number, ending on Stellar because that is what the page is for.

The honest caveat. Read strictly, 1 then 2 implies you reach Stellar via another chain. You do not have to — Ethereum can mint straight to Stellar, and any chain reaches any other directly. The caption says so, because the drawing on its own would not.
