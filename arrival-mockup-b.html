Mockup B — the USDT0 supply chain Not the page

Backed on Ethereum, minted on Stellar

A supply chain with one source. Every USDT0 in existence is backed by USDT locked on Ethereum, and reaching Stellar takes one hop, not two — either straight from Ethereum, or from any chain that already holds USDT0. Both arrive through the same contract.

Proposed — B
The correction. The previous version put “any USDT0 chain” between Ethereum and Stellar, which read as a required stop. It is not — Ethereum mints straight to Stellar. Both routes now arrive at Stellar’s OFT, which is the one Stellar-side entry point and the contract listed under Deploy.

What each part is doing. Ethereum holds the backing and is the only place USDT is locked. The other chains are a stack, not a step — a set of peers, each with its own OFT. The dashed line says the routes run both ways without labouring it.

The motion is the explanation. One 12.5-second cycle, one journey. A single marker leaves Ethereum, forks into two mints that land on the same frame, the far-chain half climbs into the Stellar OFT, and one marker carries on — which is the “one supply” point made without a caption. The two fork legs were drawn to equal length on purpose so they arrive together. Every leg runs at the same 55 px/s; each duration is that path’s measured length over that rate. Boxes light as they receive.
